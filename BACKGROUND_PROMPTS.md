# God's Pandora — Tab Background Prompts (v2: story, emotion, detail)

**Model:** FLUX.2 klein 4B · **Size:** 1536 × 864 (16:9) · steps 6–8 · guidance ~3.5 · then Upscayl 2× → 3072 × 1728
**Save as:** `src/assets/bg/<file>.jpg`. Each prompt is complete, so copy the whole block.

Every frame is built the same way:
- **One story moment.** Something just happened, or is about to happen.
- **One clear emotion.** It comes from what that tab does in the app.
- **Foreground, middle and background layers** for depth.
- **Motivated light.** Every light comes from a real source in the scene, with a warm-against-cold contrast.
- **The subject sits in the left or right third, so the centre stays dark and calm.** The UI sits in the middle.
